# Task 1 — Identify Constraints

## C1 — Barrier Must Not Open While Train Is Present

**Constraint:**
The barrier must not open while a train is present at the crossing.

**Reason:**
Opening the barrier while a train is present could allow road traffic to enter the crossing and cause an accident.

---

## C2 — Barrier Must Close Before Train Enters

**Constraint:**
The barrier must close before the train enters the crossing.

**Reason:**
The closed barrier prevents road vehicles and pedestrians from entering the crossing before the train arrives.

---

## C3 — Warning Lights Must Activate

**Constraint:**
Warning lights must activate when a train approaches the crossing.

**Reason:**
Warning lights inform road users that a train is approaching and they must stop.

---

## C4 — Audible Alarm Must Activate

**Constraint:**
The audible alarm must activate when a train approaches the crossing.

**Reason:**
The audible alarm provides an additional warning to drivers and pedestrians.

---

## C5 — Barrier Must Remain Closed While Train Is Passing

**Constraint:**
The barriers must remain closed while the train is passing through the crossing.

**Reason:**
Keeping the barriers closed prevents road traffic from entering the crossing while the train is passing.

---

## C6 — Barrier Opens Only After Train Clearance

**Constraint:**
The barrier may open only after the system has confirmed that the train has completely cleared the crossing.

**Reason:**
Opening the barrier before complete train clearance could allow traffic to enter while the train is still present.

---

## C7 — Road Traffic Must Not Enter a Closed Crossing

**Constraint:**
Road traffic must not be allowed to enter the crossing when the barriers are closed.

**Reason:**
This prevents vehicles and pedestrians from bypassing the safety barrier and entering a dangerous area.

---

## C8 — Sensor Failure Must Result in a Safe State

**Constraint:**
If an important train-detection sensor fails, the system must enter a safe state.

**Reason:**
A failed sensor may provide incorrect information, so the system must prevent unsafe crossing operation.

---

## C9 — Communication Loss Must Not Cause Barrier Opening

**Constraint:**
Loss of communication with the control center must not cause the barrier to open.

**Reason:**
During communication failure, the system may not receive correct information, so it must remain in a safe condition.

---

## C10 — Emergency Conditions Must Maintain Safety

**Constraint:**
During an emergency condition, the system must keep the crossing in a safe state.

**Reason:**
Emergency conditions may create unexpected risks, so the system must prioritize the safety of road users and railway operations.
